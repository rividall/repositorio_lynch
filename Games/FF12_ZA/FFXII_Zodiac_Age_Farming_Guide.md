# Final Fantasy XII: The Zodiac Age — Farming Guide

## Part 1: Stealing Powerful Weapons & Gear

The core idea: many rare game enemies and Trial Mode stages let you steal weapons and equipment far stronger than anything available in shops at that point. If you put in the effort, you can break the game's difficulty curve wide open.

### General Stealing Tips

- Only **Vaan**, **Balthier**, and **Fran** can learn the Steal technick.
- Equip **Thief's Cuffs** on your stealer to increase the chance of landing rare steals.
- Set your entire active party's top gambit to **Foe: Steal** when farming, so all three attempt it before anyone starts attacking.
- You can save scum Trial Mode: save before entering, steal what you want, quit out, and your main game save keeps the loot.

### Early Game (Before Raithwall's Tomb)

#### Gladius — from Lindbur Wolf
- **Location:** Dalmasca Westersand, Shimmering Horizons (isolated northeast section via Galatea Downs)
- **How to spawn:** Kill 20 wolves anywhere in the Westersand. Chaining not required but helps you keep count.
- **Steal:** Gladius (rare steal, only item in its steal pool)
- **Why it matters:** Strongest dagger available at this stage. Ideal for Shikari.
- **Tips:** Cast Sleep or Blind on the wolf so your party can steal in peace. Use the zone-out trick to respawn and steal multiple copies.

#### Heavy Lance — from Fideliant
- **Location:** Dalmasca Westersand, The Midfault
- **How to spawn:** 20% chance to appear each time you enter the zone. Available as solo Vaan before Penelo leaves.
- **Steal:** Heavy Lance
- **Why it matters:** Solid early spear for Uhlan. Way ahead of the curve for that point in the story.
- **Tips:** Grab Phoenix Downs from Nalbina Fortress first to survive the trip.

### Trial Mode (Accessible Anytime)

Trial Mode is the single biggest source of overpowered early gear. You enter, steal from enemies or open chests, then quit back to the main game keeping everything. The game auto-saves at the start of every stage up to Stage 99, so you can quit and reload to retry steals without losing progress.

**Steal probabilities (with Thief's Cuffs):** Common = 80%, Uncommon = 30%, Rare = 6%. Without Cuffs: 55% / 10% / 3%. Always equip Thief's Cuffs when farming Trial Mode.

#### Diamond Armlet — Stage 1 Chest
- Not a weapon, but changes chest loot tables in Cerobi Steppe and for the four Epic invisible items. Worth grabbing on your first Trial Mode run.

#### Karkata — from Flowering Cactoid (Stage 3)
- **Steal:** Karkata (rare steal, 3% base / 6% with Thief's Cuffs)
- **Stats:** 97 attack power, **100% confuse on hit**
- **Why it matters:** Trivializes roughly the first 60% of the main game. The stage is so early you can retry endlessly.
- **How to farm it:** Set gambits to "Foe: status = Haste -> Steal" (the Flowering Cactoid has Haste, so this targets it specifically). If you don't get the Karkata, quit to main menu and reload the Trial Mode auto-save at Stage 3. Repeat until it drops.

### Mid-Game Steals

#### Deathbringer — from Ithuno (Barheim Passage, Special Op Sector 5)
- **Prerequisite:** Barheim Key (available after Raithwall's Tomb)
- **How to spawn:** 20% chance each time you enter Special Op Sector 5. Zone in and out repeatedly.
- **How to identify:** Ithuno disguises itself as a treasure chest with a **purple bottom and silver body** (normal chests in the area are green). Interact with it and it comes alive.
- **Steal:** Deathbringer (rare steal)
- **Stats:** 83 attack power, 12% instant KO on hit
- **Why it matters:** One of the strongest one-handed swords available at this point in the story. You can farm multiple copies by zone-resetting to respawn Ithuno.
- **Tips:** Ithuno has 23,341 HP and is weak to Holy. Equip Thief's Cuffs and steal before killing it. Cast Holy-element spells or use Holy Lance to speed up the fight if the steal fails and you need to finish it off.

#### Genji Equipment — from Gilgamesh (Lhusu Mines Hunt)

**This is missable.** Gilgamesh swaps weapons mid-fight, and after each weapon swap a new Genji piece becomes available to steal. Miss the window and that piece is gone forever in the main story.

**First Encounter (Bridge):**
- After he draws his imitation Orichalcum: steal **Genji Shield** and **Genji Gloves**

**Second Encounter (Site 7):**
- After he draws the Tournesol replica: steal **Genji Helm**
- After three weapon swaps total: steal **Genji Armor**

**Strategy:** Set everyone's top gambit to Foe: Steal after each cutscene. Turn it off once someone succeeds. Thief's Cuffs strongly recommended.

#### Scarletite — from Aspidochelon (Rare Game Turtle)
- Not a weapon directly, but a key crafting ingredient for Gemsteel at the Bazaar.
- Gemsteel feeds into endgame weapon recipes including the Tournesol.
- 40% chance the rare game spawns. Look for the blue Protect status icon. Steal until you get Scarletite, then zone out to reset.

### Late Game / Deep Trial Mode

See the full Trial Mode steal table above for the complete list. The highlights for endgame farming:

- **Stage 49 (Hashmal):** Ribbon — best accessory in the game, farmable by reloading the auto-save
- **Stage 50 (Famfrit):** Wyrmhero Blade — normally requires beating both Yiazmat and Omega Mark XII
- **Stage 62 (Biding Mantis):** Zodiac Spear — one of the best weapons, far easier to get here than from the 1% Henne Mines chest
- **Stage 64 (Humbaba Mistant):** Ultima Blade or Excalibur
- **Stage 94 (Gilgamesh + Enkidu):** Full Genji set + Masamune/Kumbha/Wyrmhero Blade — the single most valuable stage

## Part 2: Important Chests — Rare Spawns & Valuable Loot

Treasure chests in FFXII work on layered RNG. Each chest has a spawn rate (whether it appears at all), a gil-vs-item split (most chests lean heavily toward gil), and then a common-vs-rare item split (usually 95/5 in Zodiac Age, with the common result often being a Knot of Rust). Understanding this system is key to farming efficiently.

### How Chests Work

1. **Spawn rate** varies per chest (10% to 100%). Respawning chests reroll every time you zone out and back in.
2. **Gil vs. Item** split determines whether you get money or loot. Many chests have an 80% gil chance.
3. **Common vs. Rare item** is typically a 95/5 split. The common item is usually a Knot of Rust.
4. **Diamond Armlet** changes the rare item pool on certain chests. In Zodiac Age, this only matters for Cerobi Steppe and the four "Epic/Invisible" items. Equip it on your party leader.
5. **Non-respawning chests** contain their item 100% of the time but can only be opened once. Don't walk past these.

### Important: No More Forbidden Chests

The original PS2 game required you to skip certain chests to get the Zodiac Spear. This restriction is completely removed in The Zodiac Age. Open everything.

### Tomb of Raithwall (Early)

Story dungeon. Most chests here respawn except the four in the Cloister of Flame. The Demonsbane secret room from the original game was **removed** in The Zodiac Age.

#### Dispel (One-Time)
- **Zone:** Royal Passage
- **Spawn:** 100%
- Removes positive status effects from enemies. Essential for boss fights throughout the game. Don't miss this.

#### Vanish (One-Time)
- **Zone:** Royal Passage
- **Spawn:** 100%
- Makes a target invisible (immune to physical attacks). Useful for protecting squishy mages.

#### Shades of Black (One-Time)
- **Zone:** Cloister of Flame (lower floor)
- **Spawn:** 100%
- Technick that casts a random Black Magick spell without consuming MP. Lets non-mage jobs contribute magical damage for free.

#### Lamia's Tiara — from Barmuu (Rare Game)
- **Zone:** Royal Passage (waystone platform)
- **How to spawn:** Kill Seekers in the Royal Passage until you reach a battle chain of 12+, then return to the waystone platform.
- **Steal:** Lamia's Tiara (Mystic Armor 5)
- Decent mystic armor upgrade for this point in the game.

#### Blood Sword — from Seekers
- **Zone:** Royal Passage (regular enemies)
- **Steal:** Blood Sword (rare steal)
- Drains HP on hit. Set your party to steal from Seekers while chaining them for the Barmuu spawn. Two birds, one stone.

#### Other Chests
- **Cloister of Flame:** 4 non-respawning chests including Elixirs
- **Southfall Passage:** Iron Hammer (RNG, respawns)
- **Northfall Passage:** Longbow (RNG, respawns), Gillie Boots near central device

### Cerobi Steppe — Diamond Armlet Zone (Late)

Cerobi Steppe is the only area in Zodiac Age where the Diamond Armlet matters for equipment drops. Every chest here has a special Diamond Armlet loot table containing endgame weapons and armor. The catch: the odds are brutal.

**Typical Cerobi chest math:**
- Chest spawn: 10-50% per zone-in
- Gil chance: ~80%
- Item chance: ~20%
- Rare item (Diamond Armlet): ~5% of the item pool
- **Effective rate per attempt: 0.1% to 0.5%**

Use x4 speed. Clear enemies first, equip the Diamond Armlet on your leader, then farm chest routes.

#### Cleanse (One-Time)

### The Four "Epic" Invisible Items

These are secret items added in the Zodiac versions. Their chests are invisible, have absurdly low spawn rates, and require the Diamond Armlet. They respawn, so you can keep trying.

#### Seitengrat (Invisible Bow) — Skyferry Air Deck
- **Stats:** 224 Attack, 75 Evasion. The strongest weapon in the game by a wide margin. Requires no license to equip.
- **Spawn:** 1% chance for the invisible chest to appear on the topmost platform of the Air Deck
- **Item chance:** 20% the chest contains an item (not gil)
- **Seitengrat chance:** 5% of the item pool (with Diamond Armlet)
- **Overall odds per attempt: 1 in 10,000 (0.01%)**
- **How to find it:** Board the Skyferry, go to the Air Deck, walk to the top platform. If you hit an invisible wall, the chest spawned. Zone in and out repeatedly at x4 speed.
- **RNG manipulation (PS4 only, does NOT work on Steam/PC):** Create a fresh save as Reks in Nalbina at game start. Load your main save, equip Diamond Armlet on Vaan, fly to Nalbina, enter the deck, close the app. Load the Reks save, cast Cure repeatedly until you see 88, 97, then 90 healed in that exact order. Quit to title, load the Skyferry autosave. The chest will have spawned. Time the opening using the "Boonswaggled Bookkeeper" NPC on the stairs (open on his 4th climb, right as he reaches the top step).
- **Steam/PC alternative:** The RNG manipulation method above does not work reliably on the PC version because the RNG seed behaves differently. On Steam, your best option is brute force at x4 speed, or installing the Seitengrat mod from Nexus Mods that forces the invisible chest to always spawn and always contain the bow.

### Farming Tips for Chest Hunting

- **x4 speed** is your best friend. Always use it when zone-grinding for respawning chests.
- **Diamond Armlet on party leader** for Cerobi Steppe and Epic items. Remove it everywhere else (it actually makes loot worse outside Cerobi).
- **Save before opening** guaranteed one-time chests if they can contain multiple items.
- **Zone-out and back** is the standard respawn method for RNG chests. Go one screen away and return.
- **Spells and technicks from chests** are unique: once you pick one up from any location, duplicate copies elsewhere disappear. You only need to find each spell/technick once.
- **Post-Raithwall** is the big unlock point where many areas and their chests become accessible. Prioritize exploring after this story beat.
