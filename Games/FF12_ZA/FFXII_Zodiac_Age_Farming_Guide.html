▶ Cerobi Steppe (Diamond Armlet Zone) Late Chest

Cerobi Steppe is the only area in Zodiac Age where the Diamond Armlet matters for equipment drops. Every chest here has a special loot table. The catch: odds are brutal.

  • Chest spawn: 10-50% per zone-in
  • Gil chance: ~80%

How Trial Mode Farming Works

Enter Trial Mode, steal from enemies or open chests, then quit back to the main game — you keep everything. The game auto-saves at the start of every stage (up to 99), so you can reload to retry steals.

  • With Thief's Cuffs: Common = 80%, Uncommon = 30%, Rare = 6%
  • Without Cuffs: Common = 55%, Uncommon = 10%, Rare = 3%

Always equip Thief's Cuffs when farming Trial Mode.

The Four Epic Invisible Items

Secret items added in the Zodiac versions. Their chests are invisible, have absurdly low spawn rates, and require the Diamond Armlet. They respawn, so you can keep trying.

Stats: 224 ATK, 75 Evasion. Requires no license to equip.
Spawn odds breakdown:
Chest spawns on Air Deck: 1%
Chest contains an item (not gil): 20%

General Stealing Tips

  • Only Vaan, Balthier, and Fran can learn the Steal technick.
  • Equip Thief's Cuffs on your stealer to boost rare steal chance.
  • Set your entire active party's top gambit to Foe: Steal when farming, so all three attempt it before attacking.
  • Save scum Trial Mode: save before entering, steal, quit out — main save keeps the loot.

Chest Hunting Tips

  • x4 speed is your best friend. Always use it when zone-grinding.
  • Diamond Armlet on party leader for Cerobi Steppe and Epic items. Remove it everywhere else (it makes loot worse outside Cerobi).
  • Save before opening guaranteed one-time chests if they can contain multiple items.
  • Zone-out and back is the standard respawn method for RNG chests. One screen away and return.
  • Spells and technicks from chests are unique: once you pick one up, duplicates elsewhere disappear.
  • Post-Raithwall is the big unlock point. Many areas and chests become accessible. Prioritize exploring after this story beat.

How Chests Work

  • Spawn rate varies per chest (10% to 100%). Respawning chests reroll every time you zone out.
  • Gil vs. Item split: many chests have an 80% gil chance.
  • Common vs. Rare item: typically 95/5 split. Common is usually a Knot of Rust.
  • Diamond Armlet changes the rare item pool. In Zodiac Age, only matters for Cerobi Steppe and the four Epic invisible items.
  • Non-respawning chests always contain their item but can only be opened once. Don't walk past these.

No More Forbidden Chests

The original PS2 game required skipping certain chests to get the Zodiac Spear. This restriction is completely removed in The Zodiac Age. Open everything.
